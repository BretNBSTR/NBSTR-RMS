# Product Overview

## Mission
Deliver a reliable, intuitive retail management system that empowers users to manage inventory, orders, and reporting with confidence.

## Vision
Create a scalable, secure platform that simplifies operations and supports growth through seamless workflows and data-driven insights.

## Design Principles
- User-centric: prioritize ease of use and clarity.
- Modular: build components that can be adapted and extended.
- Secure: protect data and access at every layer.
- Scalable: support increasing volume without sacrificing performance.
- Maintainable: keep implementation transparent and easy to update.

## Modules
- Authentication and access control
- Inventory management
- Order processing
- Reporting and analytics
- Administration and configuration

## Workflows
- User login and role-based access
- Product and inventory lifecycle management
- Order creation, fulfillment, and tracking
- Reporting generation and review
- System configuration and maintenance

## Roadmap
- Phase 1: Establish core inventory, order, and authentication functionality.
- Phase 2: Add reporting, analytics, and administrative tools.
- Phase 3: Improve performance, integrations, and scalability.
- Phase 4: Refine user experience and support advanced workflows.

## Glossary
- MVP: Minimum Viable Product
- API: Application Programming Interface
- SKU: Stock Keeping Unit
- RMS: Retail Management System
- Workflow: A defined sequence of tasks or steps within the product.
